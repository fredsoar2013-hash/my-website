Why is Linux better than Windows?
A guide

An overview

Mac Vs Windows, split down the middle, productivity against customisation. Luxury against upgradability. Well, what if there was another option? That's right, Linux! Linux is the third main operating system, featuring deep customisation and can be put on virtually anything. You may've never heard of it, but in reality, it's right under your nose… Android? Linux. TV boxes? Most are Linux. Servers? The whole internet? Linux. It may go unnoticed, but without it, the world would be very different.

Why not Windows?

Windows is recognisable. The icon, the search function, everything. You want that experience, but are fed up with AI bloat? Many users suffer the same problem, and the answer is Linux. Yes, it is different, but it has separate 'distros', like flavours of ice cream. The closest ones to Windows, just to dip your feet in, are ZorinOS and Linux Mint. You can definitely get ultra-nerd ones like gentoo or LFS, but no matter what, you will always be able to make Linux however you want. Most surprisingly, though, in some scenarios / games, Linux is on par, or even better than Windows 11!

A deeper look…

In Linux, you can do virtually anything, whether that be changing the kernel (talks to your cpu to translate what your OS needs) for better performance; install different desktop environments for different experiences; modify the source code, and even revive old laptops that Windows doesn't support. You can even read disks formatted for Windows when Windows can't read Linux disks. Possibly the most surprising: you can run some Linux distros on a USB stick! No need for an ssd or hdd when you have an 8gb flash drive! *sigh*
